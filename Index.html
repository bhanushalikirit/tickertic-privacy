Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

TickerTic ("we", "our", "the app") is a market data and financial news application for Indian stock markets. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

1. Data We Collect

Account information: If you sign in with Google, we store your email address and display name to identify your account. You may also use the app without signing in (guest mode), in which case no personal information is collected.

Watchlist and preferences: We store the stocks, indices, and instruments you add to your watchlist, your alert preferences, and notification settings. For guest users, this data is stored locally on your device only.

Device information: We generate a random device identifier (UUID) to register your device for push notifications. We also collect your FCM (Firebase Cloud Messaging) token to deliver notifications.

Usage data: We do not collect analytics, browsing history, or usage patterns.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide app functionality: displaying your watchlist, sending price alerts and news notifications you have opted into, and syncing your preferences across sessions. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond core app functionality.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. We use the following services to operate the app:

Firebase (Google): Authentication (Google Sign-In) and push notifications (FCM). Governed by Google's privacy policy.

Supabase: Database hosting for account data, watchlists, and preferences. Data is stored securely with encryption at rest.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Database access is restricted and requires authentication.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as you use the app. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by submitting a request through the app or by contacting us. Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

TickerTic is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at swifttechnology03@gmail.com.
